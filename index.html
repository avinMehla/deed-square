    About DeedSquare

    A property documentation and verification practice run by Advocate Sahil Khenchi in Panipat.

    Property is usually the largest purchase a family makes, and most disputes start with a paper nobody checked. DeedSquare was set up to deal with that early: verify the title, draft the deed properly, and see it through registration and mutation.

    We work on sale deeds, agreements to sell, gift deeds, wills, powers of attorney and affidavits, and on the legal verification that sits behind them. The advice is plain and specific, so you can decide with the facts in front of you.
